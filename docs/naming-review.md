# Earmark — historical provisional-name research

> Earmark is the approved public name as of Stage 6.5. The research below is historical; references to other Flint projects and the original provisional decision are preserved, not current Earmark branding.

Research dates: **7–8 October 2026 (Africa/Lagos)**. Repository and packages retain
**Flint**. This is a preliminary public-web conflict screen, not trademark, domain,
company-name, token-ticker, or social-handle clearance.

## Flint conflicts

Primary project sources show substantial overlap with existing projects:

- [FLINT agent passports](https://flint.network/build) describes agent identity,
  protected x402 payments, spending caps, and a kill switch.
- [Flint payments](https://withflintpay.com/) offers an AI-native payment API.
- [Flint agent marketplace](https://theflint.in/) handles agent discovery,
  monetization, and payments.
- [Flint smart-contract language paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.06534) shows
  earlier use in smart-contract security.

Confusion is plausible in the project's market. No public rename was performed.

Engineering shortlist for further human review: **Escrowisp** and **Spendweft**,
based on lower observed overlap, not clearance. Mandatide is conceptually apt but
has a crowded neighboring brand space. All five remain proposals only.

## Search method and limits

For each candidate, searched the quoted exact name alone and with `AI`, `agent`,
`crypto`, and `payments`; reviewed results for exact and adjacent-market use.
Then broadened the screen to nearby roots such as Mandate, Covenant, and Pact.

Representative queries:

```text
"Mandatide"
"Mandatide" crypto payments agent
"Covenrail"
"Covenrail" AI crypto payments
"Escrowisp"
"Escrowisp" crypto OR payments OR agent
"Spendweft"
"Spendweft" crypto OR payments OR agent
"Pactquill"
"Pactquill" crypto payments agent
Mandate AI agent payments protocol official
Covenant crypto AI agent official
Pact AI agent payments protocol official
```

An initial screen also rejected [ScopeHold](https://scopehold.com/) (scoped agent
credentials) and [Pactloom](https://getpactloom.com/) (autonomous contract
intelligence). They are not part of the five-candidate shortlist.

No relevant result is **absence of evidence**, not evidence of availability.
Search engines do not comprehensively index new, private, regional, or trademarked
projects. Adjacent-name risk is an engineering assessment inferred from the
linked projects' self-descriptions, not a legal conclusion.

Before approving a name, check jurisdiction-specific trademark/company registries,
domains, package namespaces, token listings, social handles, and similar spellings
and pronunciations. Those checks remain outstanding.
